**Confidence:** high  
**Sources:** bruce, stephen, penni  
**Evidence:** bruce:0023, bruce:0027, stephen:0032, stephen:0036, penni:0031, penni:0042  
**Counter-evidence:** none  
**Opportunity:** Move away from binary 'anonymized = safe to share' assumptions toward negotiated, tiered access models (e.g., restricted-access with data-service mediation) that account for residual identifiability risk.

**Confidence:** medium  
**Sources:** michelle, penni  
**Evidence:** michelle:0054, michelle:0070, penni:0040, penni:0042  
**Counter-evidence:** david:0036, david:0038 — David's practice of offering a revisited, tailored consent 'menu' (closed/open/negotiated) and documenting evolving participant preference suggests the problem is process-addressable rather than inherently unresolvable.  
**Opportunity:** Adopt granular, longitudinal consent tracking (menu of closed/open/tailored options, revisited at contact points) as standard practice rather than a single blanket consent decision at intake.
