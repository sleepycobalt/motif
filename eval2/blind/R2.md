**Counter-evidence:** stephen:0036 — Stephen's example shows some organizations accept identifiability as an acceptable working threshold once the name is withheld, suggesting anonymization standards are negotiable rather than absolute.  
**Opportunity:** Move from binary open/closed anonymization to a graduated access model (e.g., named-contact-gated restricted tiers) for datasets involving small populations, distinctive dialects, or audio-visual material.

**Confidence:** medium  
**Sources:** bruce, stephen

**Opportunity:** For commercially/politically sensitive projects, offer an alternative 'deposit product' of higher-level theoretical or methodological findings and anonymized excerpts when full raw data sharing is precluded.
