## I-01 — Confidentiality and identifiability of participants/organisations is the dominant barrier to opening qualitative data

**Sources:** david, stephen, penni, bruce  
**Evidence:** david:0022, stephen:0017, penni:0031, bruce:0023  
**Counter-evidence:** none  
**Opportunity:** Develop sector-specific anonymisation and tiered/controlled-access protocols (e.g. restricted vs. safeguarded archive tiers) so sensitive qualitative data can be shared under graded access rather than a binary open/closed choice.
