## I-01 — Metadata can't restore the lost context needed to interpret data

**Counter-evidence:** bruce:0035 — Bruce reframes divergent readings of reused data as an inevitable and legitimate feature of qualitative interpretation, not simply a loss caused by missing context.  
**Opportunity:** Require a structured 'context capsule' (organizational timeline, funding/political climate, researcher positionality) as a mandatory companion artifact in deposit workflows, not an optional metadata field.
